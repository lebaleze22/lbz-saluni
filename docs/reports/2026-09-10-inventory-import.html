Inventory import from CSV and Excel

Bulk-create products, update the catalogue, and reconcile physical stock through the existing audited inventory ledger.

Implemented, tested, backed up, and running locally

How to use it

  1. Open Stock.
  2. Expand Importer le stock depuis CSV ou Excel.
  3. Download the Excel or CSV template and retain all its column headings.
  4. Fill at most 1,000 product rows, then select the completed file.
  5. Choose Vérifier le fichier to preview the result without writing data.
  6. Review created, updated, unchanged, and rejected counts.
  7. Choose Confirmer l’import atomique to commit the complete file.

The stock value is the total physical quantity counted. It is not a quantity to add. An existing balance of 10 with an imported physical stock of 7 creates an audited adjustment of −3 and leaves the balance at 7.

Template columns

Column Rule
ID produit Optional. Takes priority when updating an existing product.
SKU Matched case-insensitively and normalized to uppercase. Required for a new product.
Nom Required, up to 120 characters.
Unité Use unité, ml, or g. Unit/piece and French labels are accepted.
Prix coût FCFA Whole FCFA amount, zero or greater.
Prix vente FCFA Whole FCFA amount, zero or greater.
Seuil stock bas Non-negative quantity with at most three decimals.
Stock physique Target physical balance, non-negative, with at most three decimals.
Statut active/actif or archived/archivé. Archived products must have zero stock.

Matching and audit behavior

  • A valid product ID matches only a product in the current salon.
  • Without an ID, a unique SKU updates the matching product.
  • Without a match, the SKU is required and a new product is created.
  • Uploading the same SKU-based file again reports unchanged rows instead of duplicating products.
  • Initial quantities create stock receipt movements.
  • Changes to existing physical stock create counting-adjustment movements.
  • Stock movements remain append-only and preserve the responsible user and balance.
  • A unit cannot change after inventory movements or service-consumption rules exist.
  • A product linked to a service recipe cannot be archived by import.

File and transaction safeguards

Supported formats are CSV, XLS, and XLSX, with a 2 MB limit, one worksheet, and at most 1,000 data rows. Unknown or repeated columns, formulas, invalid quantities, invalid money, repeated target products, ambiguous SKUs, foreign IDs, and duplicated rows are rejected.

Parsing and database planning finish before mutation. The commit then locks targeted products in a stable order and applies the complete file inside one tenant-scoped transaction. Any failure rolls back product fields and stock movements together. Only Owner and Director accounts can preview, import, or download templates.
