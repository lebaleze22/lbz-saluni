SALUNI · Approved feature delivery · 10 September 2026

Product sales and stock-linked receipts

A complete point-of-sale flow for retail products, connected to inventory, client dossiers, revenue reports, and exports.

Implemented, tested, migrated, and running locally

What is available

Sale entry

Select a product and quantity, optionally link a client, then record the payment.

Automatic stock

Every completed sale creates an audited stock movement and deducts the quantity.

Receipt

A permanent printable receipt records the product, price, payment, seller, and date.

Client history

Linked purchases appear in the client dossier with their receipt and product total.

Reports

Reports separate service revenue and product revenue while retaining total turnover.

Exports

Excel contains a Ventes produits worksheet; PDF includes product revenue and details.

How to record a sale

  1. Open Ventes de produits.
  2. Select a product. Only active products with stock and a sale price are offered.
  3. Enter the quantity and optionally select the client dossier.
  4. Choose Espèces, Orange Money, or MTN MoMo.
  5. Confirm the sale, then print or retain the generated receipt.
Validate request→Lock product stock→Deduct quantity→Create movement→Create receipt

These operations run inside one database transaction. If any step fails, the whole sale is rolled back and stock remains unchanged.

Business and integrity rules

Rule Result
Insufficient stock The sale is rejected without a receipt or stock deduction.
Price changed after the form opened The stale submission is rejected so the user can review the current price.
Repeated submission The same request identifier returns its existing receipt instead of selling twice.
Two users request the last unit Database locking allows only one sale to succeed.
Client from another salon The tenant-scoped relationship is rejected.
Historical receipt Runtime users can read and create receipts; update and delete are denied.

Refunds and cancellations are outside this approved increment. A future refund feature should use compensating records so the original receipt and audit trail remain intact.

Reporting behavior

Total turnover now combines service payments and product sales. The report also exposes both values separately, groups product sales by product, and combines all supported payment methods across the two revenue sources. Service staff and service-performance metrics remain based on appointments.

The Excel export adds a dedicated Ventes produits worksheet. Each product row preserves the receipt reference, date, optional client, quantity, unit price, total, payment method, and seller. The PDF report includes the same revenue split and product sale detail in a printable summary.
