Using client management

  1. Open Clients from the navigation. Search by name, phone, or email.
  2. Use Ajouter un client to create a profile. Notes, preferences, and precautions are optional.
  3. Open the client's name to view their profile, visit count, total receipts, and paginated history.
  4. Select Enregistrer une visite from the profile to open the register with that exact client selected.
  5. Use Archiver le client to remove the profile from quick entry while keeping its history. Restore it from the archived-client filter when needed.

Segments

Visite récente means at least one non-deleted visit on or after the cutoff. À relancer means the client has visited before but has no visit on or after that cutoff. Jamais venus means no recorded non-deleted visit. The cutoff follows the Douala day boundary. Filtering prepares a follow-up list; it sends no messages.

Client identity in the register

The register now offers an explicit client selection. Two people with the same name remain separate. Updated on 9 September: the register compares normalized name, phone, email, and sex together. An exact match with contact details reuses the existing record; a contact-free match requires explicit selection. Archived matches require restoration. See the client identity update.

The quick selector includes recent profiles, with a link to search all clients. Starting a visit from a profile also works for clients outside that recent list. Archived records cannot be selected or submitted as an existing client.

Historical product usage before this feature is not reconstructed. A visit shows product usage only when consumption was recorded at the time of entry.

Implementation: client directory, profile/history, client data layer.

Using inventory and service consumption

Set up products

  1. Open Stock → Ajouter un produit.
  2. Choose a unit: individual unit, millilitre, or gram.
  3. Enter the opening quantity, low-stock threshold, and prices per stock unit.
  4. Open the product to see its movements and record subsequent restocks, outgoing quantities, or a physical count.

Connect products to services

  1. Open Consommations par prestation.
  2. Select a service, then associate products and the amount used per service.
  3. Adjust quantities or remove associations when usage changes.
  4. Record a visit normally. Its configured product usage is deducted with the visit.

If any required product is unavailable or short of stock, the transaction rolls back. It does not leave a partial visit, payment, new client, or deduction from another product. Product row locks serialize simultaneous deductions, and a database constraint prevents negative balances.

Quantities and stock alerts

Quantities support three decimal places. Arithmetic uses exact thousandths, so repeated fractional consumption does not accumulate floating-point drift. A product is flagged when its quantity is at or below the configured threshold; zero stock with a zero threshold is therefore flagged.

Corrections and archives

A physical count is the total quantity actually found, not a signed adjustment. The application calculates and records the difference. Movement records cannot be edited or deleted by the runtime database role. Fix a mistake with a new, explained correction.

A product can be archived only after its stock is zero and its service associations are removed. Its unit cannot change after movements or associations exist, preserving the meaning of historical quantities. Archived products retain their history and can be restored.

Manual “Sortie / vente de produit” records inventory quantities only. It does not add retail receipts to the service-revenue report. Product point-of-sale accounting remains a separate next feature.

Implementation: stock screens, inventory data layer, transactional stock engine, quantity arithmetic.

Existing behavior stabilized

  • Client selection: explicit identities replace name-only matching, and all four identity attributes are checked together before creating a client.
  • Archived data: clients, staff, services, and visits are explicitly filtered for quick-entry use.
  • Salon access: inactive/deleted tenants and inactive application identities are refused by the relevant guards.
  • Manager provisioning: the new-account form no longer offers an operationally unusable Manager account. The existing technical Owner staff profile is unaffected.
  • Amounts: register and fixed-salary validation reject values beyond PostgreSQL's integer capacity.
  • Time: visit input and conversion explicitly use Douala time instead of inheriting the device's timezone; incomplete/invalid date input is handled without throwing during render.
  • Mobile navigation: the expanded navigation has its own scrollable row to accommodate Clients and Stock.
  • Schema comments: account permissions and client-matching comments now reflect the implemented behavior.

RLS remains the database boundary; business writes continue through app_runtime inside a tenant transaction. New inventory records use composite foreign keys for product, service, visit, and operator relationships.

Apply to an existing development installation

The new 20260908100000_clients_inventory migration adds four optional Client columns and the Product, ServiceProduct, and StockMovement tables. It contains no rewrite or deletion of existing client/visit records.

The implementation is saved in the working tree and deployed to the local Docker stack at http://localhost:54321. The previous container was still serving the old build; rebuilding the app and tooling, applying the additive migration, and recreating the app and nginx resolved this. Authenticated checks with the existing development Owner returned HTTP 200 for /register, /clients, /inventory, and /inventory/recipes, with Clients and Stock links present on all four pages. Browser visual verification remains unavailable.

The pre-migration database backup is stored privately at docs/backups/saluni-before-clients-inventory-20260908.dump, excluded from Git and Docker build context. Other installations still require their normal update procedure. No release was published and no commit was created.

Source-based local Docker development

After retaining a recoverable backup of the intended development database, build the updated images, apply the migration through tooling, and restart the app: 

docker compose --env-file .env.local -f docker-compose.local.yml build tooling core-api
docker compose --env-file .env.local -f docker-compose.local.yml run --rm tooling npx prisma migrate deploy
docker compose --env-file .env.local -f docker-compose.local.yml up -d --no-deps --force-recreate --wait core-api nginx

For a client Mac, first publish an identified image release containing these changes, then use the existing update procedure. Running the initializer is not part of a normal update.

First operational walkthrough

  1. Open Clients and confirm existing records are present.
  2. Open a client and check their earlier visits and receipts.
  3. Add a test product in the chosen QA/development environment, with a known starting quantity and threshold.
  4. Associate it with a service and record a visit for an explicitly selected client.
  5. Confirm the stock deduction, movement operator/reason, and product history on that client.
  6. Try a quantity larger than the available stock and confirm the visit is not partially recorded.
  7. Review the screens at the salon Mac's display size before client acceptance.

Remaining cahier scope

This delivery provides the client-management and inventory foundation requested. The following requirements remain separate work:

  • Customer review history and WhatsApp follow-ups, which depend on the review/messaging modules.
  • Product retail checkout, payment receipts, and inclusion of retail revenue in financial reports.
  • Automated external stock notifications; low-stock alerts currently appear inside the stock screens.
  • Full staff administrative/pay-field editing, usable Manager/Staff workspaces, and commission calculation.
  • Visit correction/refunds with matching stock reversal, scheduled calendars, public booking, and SaaS onboarding/billing.
  • Full FR/EN product localization. The new operational screens follow the current French UI.

The next useful step is a visual walkthrough on the intended runtime, followed by retail checkout and visit correction with stock reversal. Those flows should preserve the transaction and audit guarantees established here.

Documentation remains HTML by default, as recorded in the repository preference. The earlier checkpoint report records the state before this feature work.
