Operational area 1

Client management

Directory and profiles

Owner and Director can create, search, filter, edit, archive, restore, and open client dossiers. Profiles include name, phone, email, sex, preferences, allergies, precautions, internal notes, and address details.

Identity and duplicate control

Duplicate detection compares normalized name, phone, email and sex together within one salon. Exact matches are blocked or reused; a difference in any field permits a distinct client. Archived matches must be restored.

Segmentation

Filters identify recent clients, clients to contact again, and clients who have never visited. The inactivity period is configurable and defaults to 90 days using the Douala day boundary.

Acquisition and referrals

Profiles record recommendation, search, social media, passing the salon, advertisement, other, or unknown. A recommendation can link to an existing dossier or preserve an external recommender name.

Client dossier contents

  • Contact, address, preferences, allergies, internal notes, and acquisition source.
  • Visit count, service history, staff, prices, payments, and products consumed.
  • Retail purchase history and receipt totals when the sale was linked to the client.
  • Appointment history for scheduled, confirmed, arrived, cancelled, and no-show records.
  • Completed appointments appear as visits, keeping financial history in one place.
  • An append-only activity journal preserving category, event time, author, and creation time.

Client list file transfers

CSV, XLS and XLSX files support up to 1,000 client rows and 2 MB. The workflow previews before writing, reports errors by row, skips exact duplicates, rechecks inside the commit transaction, and never overwrites an existing matching profile. Exports can include active, archived, or all clients and protect spreadsheet text values.

Use the live directory at Clients.

Operational area 2

Inventory and service consumption

Product catalogue

Products carry a name, SKU, unit, cost price, sale price, available quantity, low-stock threshold, and status. Quantities retain three decimal places.

Audited movements

Opening stock, receipts, outgoing quantities, physical counts, service consumption, and product sales record the operator, reason, delta, resulting balance, product, unit, and historical cost information.

Service recipes

Each service can consume configured product quantities. A recorded visit combines all required products and deducts them in the same transaction as the visit and payment.

Stock protection

Insufficient stock rejects the entire operation. Product row locks serialize concurrent deductions, database constraints prevent negative balances, and movement history remains append-only.

Inventory file import

CSV, XLS and XLSX import can create products, update catalogue fields, and reconcile a physical stock count. Product ID takes priority, otherwise SKU matches case-insensitively. Reimporting the same file reports unchanged rows instead of duplicating products.

Preview reports created, updated, unchanged and rejected rows. Confirmation locks target products in a stable order and commits the whole file atomically. Invalid money, quantities, formulas, columns, duplicate targets, foreign IDs and illegal archive/unit changes are rejected. The stock column represents the physical total, not an amount to add.

Use the live inventory at Stock and service recipes at Consommations par prestation.

Operational area 3

Retail product sales

Validate request→Lock stock→Deduct quantity→Record movement→Create permanent receipt
  • Sell active products that have stock and a configured selling price.
  • Choose quantity, optional client, and Cash, Orange Money, or MTN MoMo.
  • Reject insufficient stock and stale prices without a partial receipt or movement.
  • Use a request identifier so repeated submission returns the same receipt.
  • Allow only one competing sale to obtain the last available quantity.
  • Preserve immutable receipts and tenant-scoped client, seller, and stock relationships.

Reports now show total turnover as service receipts plus retail receipts while retaining separate service and product totals. Excel adds a Ventes produits worksheet, and PDF includes the same sales summary and details. Linked purchases appear in the client dossier.

Use the live sales screen at Ventes de produits.

Operational area 4

Appointment scheduling and salon calendar

Calendar

Daily and weekly views can be filtered by date, staff member, service, and status. The screen highlights upcoming and late appointments for the current day.

Booking

Create an appointment for an existing client or create a client during booking. Add services, staff, date, time, duration, and internal notes.

Lifecycle

Supported statuses are scheduled, confirmed, arrived, completed, cancelled, and no-show. Outcome totals feed weekly, monthly, and quarterly reporting.

Collision prevention

The database rejects overlapping active appointments for the same staff member, including concurrent requests. Adjacent appointments remain valid.

Arrival and checkout

An arrived appointment can be completed without re-entering the client, staff, services, time, or notes. Checkout creates the payment, consumes service recipe stock, timestamps completion, and changes the status in one transaction. Repeated completion is idempotent.

Only completed appointments contribute service revenue, visits, and client segments. Cancelled and no-show records remain visible in the client dossier and appointment outcome report. This keeps operational history without inflating financial results.

Use the live calendar at Agenda.
