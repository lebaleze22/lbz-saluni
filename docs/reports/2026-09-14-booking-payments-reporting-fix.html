SALUNI · Approved correction · 14 September 2026

Bookings, payments, stock, and reports now follow one consistent lifecycle

A future appointment no longer behaves like a completed and fully paid salon visit.

Implemented, migrated, repaired, tested, and running locally

Corrected business behavior

ActionAppointmentPaymentInventory and reports
Book a future appointment scheduled, then optionally confirmed Unpaid, partially paid by advance, or fully paid No stock consumption and no realized service revenue
Record an additional booking payment Status remains operationally independent Payment is added without exceeding the planned total Cash and advances appear by their actual receipt date
Complete checkout completed Only the remaining balance is collected Stock is consumed once and service revenue becomes realized
Record a visit already completed completed immediately Full settlement is required Stock and realized revenue are recorded immediately

User-facing changes

Register

Explicit choices for Visite réalisée and Rendez-vous à venir, with duration and an optional advance for bookings.

Appointments

Each appointment displays unpaid, partially paid, or paid state, amounts received, balance, and payment history.

Checkout

The completion form shows the exact remaining balance and supports appointments already paid in full.

Register totals

The daily card now reports actual cash received from services and retail product sales.

Period navigation

Reports include previous and next controls, making the 7–13 September week directly accessible from 14 September.

Client dossier

Appointment history includes payment state while retaining the operational appointment state.

Reporting corrections

  • All six appointment outcomes are visible in the screen, PDF, and Excel: scheduled, confirmed, arrived, completed, cancelled, and no-show.
  • Realized service revenue is based on completed services. Cash received is tracked separately by payment receipt date.
  • Advances, unpaid bookings, partial bookings, paid bookings, and the remaining open balance are displayed separately.
  • Product reports now show both the number of sale receipts and the sum of units sold.
  • Product quantities are grouped by measurement unit so units, millilitres, and grams are never combined into a misleading total.
  • The Excel export includes a product summary worksheet; PDF and Excel contain the expanded appointment and payment metrics.

Example corrected by this implementation: three Zara Red Edition sale receipts containing five units now report 3 receipts and 5 units.

Database design

Migration 20260914100000_booking_payments adds payment purpose (advance or settlement) and the actual receipt timestamp. Existing payment rows are backfilled safely, positive payment amounts are enforced, and receipt-date reporting is indexed.

Booking payments and service completion are independent. This prevents an advance from completing an appointment or consuming stock, while checkout remains idempotent and cannot consume a service recipe twice.
